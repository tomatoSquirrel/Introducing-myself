Timeline

2006 - 2007

I was born in Chapel Hill, North Carolina to Yuyang Tang and Guochun Jiang. I have an older brother named Johnny who's two years older than me. I don't remember anything from this point in my life so that's all I have for here

2007 - 2011

After I turned 1, my family moved to Nashville, Tennessee because my dad switched jobs. I had a dora haircut like most other asian children and my mom dressed me very cutesy. I was adorable but I was the brattiest brat who has every bratted in the history of brats

2011 - 2019

My dad changed jobs again and we moved to Davis, California. I have a lot more memories from this time period. Elementary school me sucked because I was still a brat, and then in 6th grade I had a breakthrough. I really miss Davis. There a thousand parks and playgrounds around, everything was very pedestrian safe, and, what I miss most, you could bike everywhere. I think I'll always want to go back and visit the places I used to go to all the time as a kid. I really want to bike the same path I would use to go to elementary school but plane tickets are expensive so not anytime soon probaby

2019 - 2024

My dad got a really good job offer and we moved back to Chapel Hill. I began middle school and I became super angsty. Then the first three years of high school I was still super angsty and ugly. Junior year I took 5 APs and AP chem made me question my existence. Senior year I began my glowup, but Physics C and college applications made me question my existence again. But I got into a decent computer science program and I graduated with a 4.0 gpa so my parents were satisfied. I also got new glasses so I glowed up hard for sure for sure

2024 - now

And, as stated before, I am now at the University of Maryland, College Park studying computer science. I've met a lot of great people and I've really enjoyed it so far. But I need to get an internship to have any hope of employment after graduation so that's partly why I'm making all these websites
